How It Works

A Simple, Reliable Pipeline

B1Connector acts like a secure post office for your business data — receiving, verifying, translating, and delivering every order automatically.

Step 1

A Customer Places an Order

A customer completes a purchase on your Shopify store. Nothing changes on their end — the checkout experience is exactly the same.

Step 2

Shopify Sends a Notification (Webhook)

Shopify instantly sends a secure notification to B1Connector containing all order details: customer info, line items, quantities, prices, and shipping address.

Step 3

B1Connector Verifies Authenticity

Before processing, B1Connector cryptographically verifies the notification is genuine — not a fake or malicious request — using industry-standard HMAC signature validation.

Step 4

Translation & Routing

B1Connector identifies which company (tenant) the store belongs to, decrypts the appropriate SAP B1 credentials, and translates the Shopify order format into SAP B1's expected structure.

Step 5

Sales Order Created in SAP B1

The translated order is sent to SAP Business One's Service Layer API, creating a complete sales order with all line items, quantities, and pricing — automatically.

Step 6

Logged & Monitored

Every step is recorded with timestamps. If anything fails (e.g., SAP B1 is temporarily offline), the system automatically retries and logs the error for your review.

Features

Built for Business

Every feature is designed with security, scalability, and ease of use in mind.

🏪

Multi-Store Support

Connect as many Shopify stores as you need. Each store's data, credentials, and logs are completely isolated for security and compliance.

🔐

Enterprise-Grade Security

All API keys, webhook secrets, and SAP B1 passwords are encrypted with AES-256 before storage. Plain-text credentials never touch the database.

📊

Live Dashboard

Monitor every sync job in real time. See success rates, pending jobs, and detailed logs. Separate admin and client views for fine-grained access control.

🔄

Automatic Retry

If SAP B1 is temporarily unavailable, B1Connector automatically retries failed jobs. No orders are ever lost — they stay in the queue until successfully processed.

🔌

Designed to Grow

While Shopify is the first connector, B1Connector is architected so new platforms — WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce — can be added without rebuilding the system.

📋

Complete Audit Trail

Every action is timestamped and logged: when the order arrived, when it was processed, which system it was sent to, and whether it succeeded or failed.
